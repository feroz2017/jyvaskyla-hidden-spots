      Before you go

      Finnish nature has a few simple rules.

      Everyman's rights

      You can walk, cycle, ski, and pick berries and mushrooms almost anywhere in Finnish nature, as long as you don't disturb people or damage anything. Keep clear of private yards.

      Campfires

      Light fires only at marked fire places. When a forest fire warning is in effect, open fires are banned even there. Check the Finnish Meteorological Institute before you go.

      Leave no trace

      Carry out everything you bring in. Many laavus and fire pits are kept up by the city or volunteers, so leave them tidier than you found them.

      Read the season

      Summer brings long light evenings and mosquitoes. Autumn brings ruska, the fall colours. In winter, dress in layers and never walk on lake ice unless you know it's safe.
